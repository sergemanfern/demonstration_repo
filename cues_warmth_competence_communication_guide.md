# Cues: Words to Communicate Warmth and Competence
## A Complete Guide Based on Vanessa Van Edwards' Research

## Executive Summary
"Cues" by Vanessa Van Edwards reveals that people are judged on two critical dimensions: warmth and competence. This guide provides specific words, phrases, and language patterns that effectively communicate both qualities, helping you build trust and influence in any communication context.

## The Two Dimensions of Influence

### 1. Warmth (Trust)
**Definition**: How friendly, approachable, and trustworthy you appear
**Perception**: "Do I like this person? Can I trust them?"
**Impact**: Determines if people want to work with you

### 2. Competence (Respect)
**Definition**: How capable, intelligent, and skilled you appear
**Perception**: "Can this person do what they say? Are they capable?"
**Impact**: Determines if people believe you can deliver

## Language Patterns to Avoid

### 1. Words That Undermine Warmth
**Cold or Distant Language**:
- "I don't care"
- "That's not my problem"
- "I'm too busy"
- "I don't have time"
- "That's not my job"

**Dismissive Language**:
- "Whatever"
- "I don't know"
- "I can't help you"
- "That's not possible"
- "I won't do that"

### 2. Words That Undermine Competence
**Uncertainty Language**:
- "I think maybe"
- "I'm not sure"
- "I might be able to"
- "I could try"
- "I'll do my best"

**Weak Language**:
- "I'm just"
- "I'm only"
- "I'm kind of"
- "I'm sort of"
- "I'm a little bit"

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### 1. Overdoing One Quality
**Too Much Warmth, Not Enough Competence**:
- People like you but don't respect your abilities
- You're seen as friendly but not capable
- Others don't trust you with important tasks
- You're included socially but not professionally

**Too Much Competence, Not Enough Warmth**:
- People respect you but don't like you
- You're seen as capable but cold
- Others don't want to work with you
- You're respected but not trusted

### 2. Inconsistent Communication
**Mixed Messages**:
- Saying warm things but acting coldly
- Demonstrating competence but being unfriendly
- Inconsistent behavior across situations
- Different communication styles with different people

**Context Mismatch**:
- Using warm language in formal situations
- Using formal language in casual settings
- Not adapting to different audiences
- Ignoring cultural or situational context
