# Cues + Magic Words: French Tech Services Copy Guide
## Combining Jonah Berger's Insights with French Cultural Sensibility

## Executive Summary
This guide combines the psychological insights from "Cues" and "Magic Words" by Jonah Berger with French cultural preferences to create compelling, sensual copy that sells tailored tech services. Learn how to use psychological triggers, cultural sensitivity, and structured arguments to demonstrate undeniable value to French customers.

## Core Insights from Jonah Berger's Books

### 1. Cues: The Hidden Psychology of Influence
**Key Principles**:
- **Social Proof**: People follow what others are doing
- **Authority**: Expertise and credentials build trust
- **Scarcity**: Limited availability increases desire
- **Reciprocity**: Giving value creates obligation to return
- **Commitment**: Small commitments lead to larger ones

**Application to French Market**:
- **Cultural Authority**: French respect intellectual and cultural authority
- **Social Validation**: French value community and cultural recognition
- **Exclusive Access**: French appreciate limited, sophisticated offerings
- **Reciprocal Value**: French appreciate mutual benefit and cultural exchange

### 2. Magic Words: What to Say to Get Your Way
**Key Principles**:
- **Specificity**: Vague language reduces effectiveness
- **Concreteness**: Tangible benefits beat abstract concepts
- **Emotional Resonance**: Connect with feelings and desires
- **Social Connection**: Use "we" and "together" language
- **Future Focus**: Paint pictures of desired outcomes

**Application to French Market**:
- **Intellectual Sophistication**: Use sophisticated, nuanced language
- **Cultural Context**: Reference French cultural values and achievements
- **Aesthetic Appeal**: Emphasize beauty and elegance in solutions
- **Long-term Vision**: Focus on lasting cultural and business impact

## French Customer Psychology and Preferences

### 1. Cultural Values That Drive Decisions
**Intellectual Sophistication**:
- French customers value complex, well-reasoned arguments
- They appreciate intellectual depth and cultural context
- They respect expertise and sophisticated understanding
- They value aesthetic quality and elegant presentation

**Cultural Pride and Heritage**:
- French customers take pride in their cultural achievements
- They value traditions and established excellence
- They appreciate recognition of French cultural contributions
- They respect cultural sensitivity and understanding

**Quality and Craftsmanship**:
- French customers expect exceptional quality and attention to detail
- They value precision, reliability, and sophisticated solutions
- They appreciate long-term relationships and partnerships
- They respect proven track records and established expertise

### 2. Communication Preferences
**Language and Tone**:
- **Formal**: Use "vous" and formal business language
- **Sophisticated**: Employ sophisticated vocabulary and concepts
- **Cultural**: Reference French cultural and intellectual traditions
- **Respectful**: Show appreciation for French business practices

**Argument Structure**:
- **Logical**: Present arguments in clear, logical sequence
- **Evidence-based**: Provide concrete proof and examples
- **Cultural context**: Frame benefits within French cultural values
- **Long-term thinking**: Emphasize lasting value and relationships
